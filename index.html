How the table works

Teams are ranked by league points: 3 for a win and 1 for a draw. Ties are broken by total FPL points scored this season, the same rule FPL Draft uses. While a gameweek is in progress, points and "This GW" are worked out from the live scores, so the table moves as the matches do.

Bonus points only become official when FPL marks a match finished. Until then, "This GW" includes FPL's own live bonus estimate, based on each player's current BPS ranking in his match.

Prize shows what each position pays if the season ended now. The pool is 0.0201 BTC, valued at the live BTC price. First takes the pool minus $300, second takes $300, and third collects $150: $100 from last place and $50 from seventh.

What the columns mean

Form is FPL's average points per match over the last 30 days, the quickest sign of who is in a purple patch. PPG is points per match across the season, and Starts shows whether he is a regular, and Last 3 shows his minutes in each of the last three finished gameweeks (green bar 60+ minutes, red bar none), so you can spot who has been dropped or has just broken in. xGI adds up expected goals and expected assists, so a high xGI with few points suggests returns are coming. DC is defensive contributions, which earn defenders and midfielders bonus points once they pass FPL's threshold in a match.

"Pens" marks the club's first-choice penalty taker. A % or red flag is FPL's chance of him playing next; hover it for the news. "Dropped by" shows the last team that released him.

The number after a club is its current Premier League position, worked out from this season's results (points, then goal difference, then goals scored). Use the league position filter to keep only players from the stronger, or weaker, clubs.

How squad rank works

It's scored like a category league. Each squad is split into four positions, and in each one we add up the season FPL points of its best players: one goalkeeper, four defenders, four midfielders and two forwards. Teams are ranked within each position, 8 points for the best down to 1, and the order comes from adding those up.

Form adds up FPL's form for that same best eleven: each player's average points per match over the last 30 days. Fixtures is the average FPL difficulty of their next three matches, 1 easiest to 5 hardest. Injured counts every flagged player in the squad; hover it to see who.

Fixture chips are green when the average is under 2.75 and red when it's over 3.25.

Season points include anything a player scored before his current team signed him, so this measures the squad, not the results. The Table column shows where each team actually sits.

How the chance to win is worked out

Each team's likely final score is its live points plus a typical score for every starter who hasn't played yet. The more players a team still has to come, the less certain its final score, and that uncertainty is factored in. The win % is the chance one team's projected final score beats the other's.

It's a statistical estimate, not a prediction from FPL. It updates with every refresh and swings most early in a gameweek, when few players have played.

Fixtures: this gameweek's match, then next gameweek's. Capitals are home games, lower case away, coloured by FPL difficulty; a struck-through grey chip has already been played. A % next to a name is FPL's chance of him playing; hover it for the news.
